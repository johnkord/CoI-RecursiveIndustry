Recursive Industry changelog
============================

0.19.0c - Orbital power choice correction
-----------------------------------------

- Removed the dominated 30 MW Orbital Power Relay from new-game research.
- Kept its prototype registered only so existing pre-release saves can load.
- Orbital Breakthrough now unlocks only the 240 MW Orbital Power Array.
- Renamed the public support-duration setting for the Array and corrected its
  one-Dossier-per-cycle description.

0.19.0b - Pre-release source preparation
----------------------------------------

- Published the curated mod source, original art, generators, and economy model.
- Added public build, compatibility, contribution, security, and verification docs.
- Renamed the visible Epoch V subtitle from Self-Expanding Frontier to Recursive
  Frontier to describe bounded reinvestment without implying automatic expansion.
- Gameplay vectors remain those of the 0.19.0a integrated author candidate.

0.19.0a - Integrated author candidate
-------------------------------------

- Added nineteen specialist universal-industry facilities and five research
  branches.
- Added 234 Direct bindings, ten Integrated recipes, and ten Precision recipes.
- Added supported-abundance policies, autonomous mobility improvements, and the
  240 MW Orbital Power Array.
- Rebalanced Computing and Package demand while preserving 415 MW of Direct
  process power.
- Added original visual identities for all new universal facilities.
