Recursive Industry changelog
============================

0.22.0a - Circular Agrifood candidate
-------------------------------------

- Added four Stream-free authored agrifood rows without changing 235 Direct,
  21 Integrated, or 10 Precision recipes.
- Added Feed-hydrolysate routes for formulated Eggs, serum-free cultured Meat,
  and mycoprotein Meat Trimmings. All retain Oxygen, water, CO2, Waste Water,
  power, and formulation inputs.
- Added Companion Provisions and an optional staffed Companion Animal Center.
  At full satisfaction it grants 0.6 Unity, consumes 0.02 Provisions per person
  per month, returns 0.004 Waste, and grants no Health or productivity bonus.
- Added two original identities to dependency-free uiicons_5287.
- Preserved poultry, compost, organic fertilizer, steam, and burning as the
  existing value ladder for surplus Animal Feed. No gas-fed Feed route was added.

0.21.0d - Native farm upgrade candidate
----------------------------------------

- Linked Greenhouse II to Sensor-Guided Greenhouse and Chicken Farm to
  Monitored Poultry Farm through the native in-place upgrade system.
- Preserved entity IDs, crop and flock state, layouts, ports, maintenance, and
  operating vectors. Native differential costing charges only the added
  Electronics IV, Packages, and Programs.

0.21.0c - Port-layout-corrected Adaptive Agrifood candidate
------------------------------------------------------------

- Fixed the 0.21.0b General Manufacturing registration failure by placing its
  one remaining Industrial Control Stream input on the proven top edge of the
  unchanged seven-row Chemical Plant II shell.
- Preserved all recipe vectors, owners, and the exact 235 Direct bindings.

0.21.0b - Registration-corrected Adaptive Agrifood candidate
--------------------------------------------------------------

- This candidate resolved the Electronics II source, then failed registration
  on the General Manufacturing port union. Use 0.21.0d or later.
- Fixed the 0.21.0a registration failure in Integrated Electronics III by
  resolving its composition-only Electronics II source from Assembly V.
- Preserved all recipe quantities, durations, machine ownership, and the exact
  235 Direct binding inventory.

0.21.0a - Adaptive Agrifood candidate
-------------------------------------

- This candidate failed registration before world creation and is preserved as
  negative evidence. Use 0.21.0d or later.
- Added five-step Precision Irrigation, reducing farm water demand by up to 10%
  without increasing crop yield or changing settlement water and rain collection.
- Added a four-worker Sensor-Guided Greenhouse as an exact Greenhouse II family
  clone with additional Electronics IV, Package, and Program construction cost.
- Added a four-worker Monitored Poultry Farm preserving native flock growth,
  feed, water, starvation, slaughter controls, Eggs, and Chicken Carcass.
- Added one optional Adaptive Agrifood Systems research child after Autonomous
  Essential Systems, with no duplicate lifetime-production gate.
- Added three distinct agrifood UI identities to dependency-free uiicons_5287.
- Added no crop scheduler, farm-to-Food-Pack shortcut, yield bonus, traceability
  commodity, cold-chain simulation, custom saved state, or zero-worker biology.

0.20.0a - Industrial Control and Federated Deployment candidate
----------------------------------------------------------------

- Added Data-only Access and Backbone Fiber plus conserved Industrial Control
  Stream for twelve cross-stage Integrated compositions.
- Added a local 210/minute Gateway row and a 250%-power 420/minute Backbone row,
  both preserving 210 Stream per Package.
- Added the 640-Packages/hour Deployment Assurance Campus at unchanged Model,
  Lab Equipment IV, and Electronics III ratios.
- Federated Deployment becomes available immediately after Industrial Control
  Networks and retains its 480-month Space Research cost. Redundant weaker
  lifetime gates were removed because Epoch V already requires 256 Programs.
- Added production-scale Electronics III and kept all Direct and Precision rows
  Fiber-free.
- Added five Stream-controlled directed refinery slates for Diesel, gaseous
  fuels, Hydrogen, Plastic, and Rubber. Polymer pays 300% recipe power and
  Elastomer pays 400%; all intermediate oil fractions are consumed internally.

0.19.0d - Complete Relay removal
--------------------------------

- Removed the 30 MW Orbital Power Relay prototype and building ID entirely.
- Orbital Breakthrough and the power toolbar now expose only the 240 MW Array.
- Pre-release saves containing a placed Relay are not compatible with this
  version; start a new campaign or remove the Relay before upgrading.

0.19.0c - Orbital power choice correction
-----------------------------------------

- Removed the dominated 30 MW Orbital Power Relay from new-game research.
- Retained its prototype temporarily for existing pre-release saves.
- Orbital Breakthrough now unlocks only the 240 MW Orbital Power Array.
- Renamed the public support-duration setting for the Array and corrected its
  one-Dossier-per-cycle description.

0.19.0b - Pre-release source preparation
----------------------------------------

- Published the curated mod source, original art, generators, and economy model.
- Added public build, compatibility, contribution, security, and verification docs.
- Renamed the visible Epoch V subtitle from Self-Expanding Frontier to Recursive
  Frontier to describe bounded reinvestment without implying automatic expansion.
- Gameplay vectors remain those of the 0.19.0a integrated author candidate.

0.19.0a - Integrated author candidate
-------------------------------------

- Added nineteen specialist universal-industry facilities and five research
  branches.
- Added 234 Direct bindings, ten Integrated recipes, and ten Precision recipes.
- Added supported-abundance policies, autonomous mobility improvements, and the
  240 MW Orbital Power Array.
- Rebalanced Computing and Package demand while preserving 415 MW of Direct
  process power.
- Added original visual identities for all new universal facilities.
