Recursive Industry changelog
============================

0.20.0a - Industrial Control and Federated Deployment candidate
----------------------------------------------------------------

- Added Data-only Access and Backbone Fiber plus conserved Industrial Control
  Stream for twelve cross-stage Integrated compositions.
- Added a local 210/minute Gateway row and a 250%-power 420/minute Backbone row,
  both preserving 210 Stream per Package.
- Added the 640-Packages/hour Deployment Assurance Campus at unchanged Model,
  Lab Equipment IV, and Electronics III ratios.
- Federated Deployment becomes available immediately after Industrial Control
  Networks and retains its 480-month Space Research cost. Redundant weaker
  lifetime gates were removed because Epoch V already requires 256 Programs.
- Added production-scale Electronics III and kept all Direct and Precision rows
  Fiber-free.
- Added five Stream-controlled directed refinery slates for Diesel, gaseous
  fuels, Hydrogen, Plastic, and Rubber. Polymer pays 300% recipe power and
  Elastomer pays 400%; all intermediate oil fractions are consumed internally.

0.19.0d - Complete Relay removal
--------------------------------

- Removed the 30 MW Orbital Power Relay prototype and building ID entirely.
- Orbital Breakthrough and the power toolbar now expose only the 240 MW Array.
- Pre-release saves containing a placed Relay are not compatible with this
  version; start a new campaign or remove the Relay before upgrading.

0.19.0c - Orbital power choice correction
-----------------------------------------

- Removed the dominated 30 MW Orbital Power Relay from new-game research.
- Retained its prototype temporarily for existing pre-release saves.
- Orbital Breakthrough now unlocks only the 240 MW Orbital Power Array.
- Renamed the public support-duration setting for the Array and corrected its
  one-Dossier-per-cycle description.

0.19.0b - Pre-release source preparation
----------------------------------------

- Published the curated mod source, original art, generators, and economy model.
- Added public build, compatibility, contribution, security, and verification docs.
- Renamed the visible Epoch V subtitle from Self-Expanding Frontier to Recursive
  Frontier to describe bounded reinvestment without implying automatic expansion.
- Gameplay vectors remain those of the 0.19.0a integrated author candidate.

0.19.0a - Integrated author candidate
-------------------------------------

- Added nineteen specialist universal-industry facilities and five research
  branches.
- Added 234 Direct bindings, ten Integrated recipes, and ten Precision recipes.
- Added supported-abundance policies, autonomous mobility improvements, and the
  240 MW Orbital Power Array.
- Rebalanced Computing and Package demand while preserving 415 MW of Direct
  process power.
- Added original visual identities for all new universal facilities.
