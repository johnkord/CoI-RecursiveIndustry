Recursive Industry changelog
============================

0.27.0a - Complete World Artwork (unpublished)
----------------------------------------------

- Completes original Fiber profiles and hardware, six later cargo products,
  three installed rack generations, eight vehicles, eight load attachments,
  and seventeen locomotive/tender/nuclear-consist models.
- Preserves native rig and working-state controls, load eligibility and offsets,
  headlights, train numbers, wheel/track motion, and tender/dump fill animation.
- Preserves all gameplay/config values, existing gameplay IDs, 52 building models,
  and 57 prior bundles. Three scoped amphibious attachment IDs isolate its art.
- Adds final-bundle visual, rig, shader-control, source-equivalence and negative
  checks. Native shared game infrastructure and audio are intentionally reused.
- Implementation completion does not imply in-game acceptance, save migration,
  independent testing, stable release, or publication.

0.26.0a - Complete Building Artwork (unpublished)

- Inherits reported Civic success and acceptance of the earlier model direction.
  New-art visual feedback remains open; no persistence, general save-migration,
  independent-test, or stable-release claim is made.

0.25.0a - Island Reconstruction and Civic Knowledge (unpublished)
----------------------------------------------------------------

- Moves terrestrial Materials, Process, Essential, and Advanced portfolios to
  Epoch III and removes redundant Program and Space gates. Direct recipes retain
  native locks; source equipment and composed technologies remain prerequisites.
- Keeps Nuclear at Epoch IV with Calibration. Moves Orbital Fabrication and
  Integrated Crew Provisioning to Epoch IV so they do not delay terrestrial work.
- Makes Adaptive and Circular Agrifood ordinary research after Essential Systems.
- Adds Civic Knowledge Stream, Civic Model Center, Knowledge Commons, and one
  optional research branch. The staffed service provides Unity only while supplied.
- Adds four original world models and three civic icons with complete bundle
  dependencies. Existing process vectors, layouts, Focus outputs, and native
  behavior remain unchanged.
- Preserves the exact 0.24.0a bridge archive and source snapshot. No migration,
  stable-release, or completed in-game testing claim is made.

0.20.0a - Industrial Control and Federated Deployment candidate
----------------------------------------------------------------

- Added Data-only Access and Backbone Fiber plus conserved Industrial Control
  Stream for twelve cross-stage Integrated compositions.
- Added a local 210/minute Gateway row and a 250%-power 420/minute Backbone row,
  both preserving 210 Stream per Package.
- Added the 640-Packages/hour Deployment Assurance Campus at unchanged Model,
  Lab Equipment IV, and Electronics III ratios.
- Federated Deployment becomes available immediately after Industrial Control
  Networks and retains its 480-month Space Research cost. Redundant weaker
  lifetime gates were removed because Epoch V already requires 256 Programs.
- Added production-scale Electronics III and kept all Direct and Precision rows
  Fiber-free.
- Added five Stream-controlled directed refinery slates for Diesel, gaseous
  fuels, Hydrogen, Plastic, and Rubber. Polymer pays 300% recipe power and
  Elastomer pays 400%; all intermediate oil fractions are consumed internally.
