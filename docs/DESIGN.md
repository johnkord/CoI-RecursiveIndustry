# Design

## Player promise

Recursive Industry extends a mature Captain of Industry island with an expensive
cybernetic layer. Intelligence is represented through physical compute hardware,
data curation, model development, experiments, validated deployment artifacts,
power, cooling, maintenance, logistics, and workers.

The player does not manufacture a magical intelligence commodity. They build and
operate the institutions and infrastructure needed to make automation reliable.

## Design pillars

### Make AI physical

Every major benefit must retain a visible material, energy, maintenance,
validation, workforce, or logistical cost. Higher capability displaces
bottlenecks; it does not erase the economy.

### Extend vanilla systems

The mod begins after vanilla Computing and advanced research. It reuses standard
Data Centers, machine behavior, product logistics, vehicles, trains, policies,
world contracts, and research rather than replacing the simulation with a custom
parallel game.

### Preserve choices and fallbacks

Conventional factories remain available. Autonomous facilities specialize in
throughput, integration, or feedstock efficiency while paying in capital,
Computing, and power. A modded path should win under a recognizable constraint,
not in every state.

### Validate before deployment

Dataset Archives feed Model Archives. Models, experiments, and physical trials
feed renewable Validated Control Packages and Research Dossiers. Validation is a
production economy, not flavor text.

### Keep recursion bounded

The late game supports recursive **reinvestment**, not literal self-replication.
Programs and capital produce Frontier Projects; a Project can build an Integration
Array that accelerates future Programs or a Construction Nexus that expands
capital-goods capacity. Every iteration still requires raw matter, energy,
maintenance, electronics, validation, logistics, and player decisions.

## Deliberate non-goals

- No free material, positive recycling, or perpetual production loop.
- No global machine-speed or reactor-output multiplier.
- No automatic building placement, terrain expansion, or factory duplication.
- No custom dispatch replacement for native trucks and trains.
- No requirement to abandon conventional machines.
- No custom runtime networking or telemetry.
- No low-power recipe family; efficient modes intentionally spend more energy.

## Why the name

“Recursive Industry” refers to the bounded reinvestment loop and to advanced
Computing improving the industrial process that supports subsequent Computing,
research, and expansion. Most operation remains ordinary factory planning; the
recursive payoff is the culmination rather than a claim of autonomous
self-replication.
