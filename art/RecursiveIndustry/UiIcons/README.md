# Recursive Industry UI icons

This directory contains original icon source and reviewed exports for later
products, machines, offices, vehicles, trains, power entities, and Focus policies.

- `masters/`: high-resolution source images.
- `exports/`: reviewed 512x512 RGBA Unity imports.
- `proofs/`: compact-size and grayscale review sheets.
- `asset-manifest.json`: paths, hashes, and bundle inventory.

The exports are shipped in dependency-free bundle `uiicons_5287`. They use a flat
industrial symbol language designed for Captain of Industry's compact research and
recipe interfaces.

These files are covered by COI-Open and may only be reused within the Captain of
Industry ecosystem under that license and the official Modding Policy.
